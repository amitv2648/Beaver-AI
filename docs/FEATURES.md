# Beaver AI Features

These priorities reflect the approved high-level roadmap. They do not represent implementation status or delivery commitments.

## Must Have

- Authentication and accounts
- Student onboarding and profiles
- Courses, curricula, lessons, and educational content
- Progress and mastery tracking
- Practice and assessment
- Personalized curricula and recommendations
- AI tutoring
- Voice tutoring
- Gamification and the BBucks economy
- Production readiness, safety, and scale

## Could Have

- Interactive subject workspaces
- Multimodal learning
- Parent and guardian learning insights with privacy controls and appropriate permissions

## Could Wait

- Social learning
- Leagues and leaderboards
- Academic test preparation
- College planning and career exploration
