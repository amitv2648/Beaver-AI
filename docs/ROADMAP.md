# Beaver AI Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Product & Technical Foundation

**Purpose:** Establish the product and technical foundation for Beaver AI.

**Major capabilities:** Product definition, foundational architecture, development standards, and project infrastructure.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 2 — Authentication & Accounts

**Purpose:** Provide secure access to Beaver AI through managed user accounts.

**Major capabilities:** Account creation, sign-in, account management, and access control.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 3 — Student Onboarding & Profiles

**Purpose:** Establish each student's identity, goals, skill level, and learning context.

**Major capabilities:** Student onboarding, learner profiles, goals, preferences, and starting skill information.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 4 — Course & Curriculum System

**Purpose:** Organize learning into structured courses and curricula.

**Major capabilities:** Courses, curricula, learning paths, subjects, and curriculum structure.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 5 — Lessons & Educational Content

**Purpose:** Deliver structured instructional experiences and learning materials.

**Major capabilities:** Lessons, explanations, examples, learning activities, and educational content.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 6 — Progress & Mastery

**Purpose:** Track learning progress and demonstrated understanding over time.

**Major capabilities:** Progress tracking, mastery tracking, learning history, strengths, and weaknesses.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 7 — Practice & Assessment

**Purpose:** Help students practice concepts and demonstrate understanding.

**Major capabilities:** Practice activities, questions, feedback, assessments, and learning evidence.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 8 — Personalized Curriculum

**Purpose:** Adapt each student's learning path using their goals and learning evidence.

**Major capabilities:** Adaptive learning paths, personalized recommendations, and curriculum adjustments.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 9 — AI Tutor

**Purpose:** Provide personalized instruction that helps students understand concepts.

**Major capabilities:** Explanations, questions, hints, guided practice, feedback, and adaptive tutoring.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 10 — Interactive Subject Workspaces

**Purpose:** Support subject-specific learning through interactive tools.

**Major capabilities:** Interactive workspaces and specialized tools for supported learning domains.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 11 — Multimodal Learning

**Purpose:** Support learning through multiple forms of input and educational media.

**Major capabilities:** Multimodal interactions and learning experiences across supported content formats.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 12 — Voice Tutor

**Purpose:** Make voice a major interaction method for personalized tutoring.

**Major capabilities:** Spoken tutor interaction, voice input, and voice responses.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 13 — Gamification & BBucks Economy

**Purpose:** Encourage consistent, meaningful learning through motivation and rewards.

**Major capabilities:** XP, streaks, BBucks, achievements, levels, quests, challenges, and rewards.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 14 — Social Learning

**Purpose:** Enable students to learn and engage with others.

**Major capabilities:** Social learning, leagues, and leaderboards.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 15 — Parent & Guardian Experience

**Purpose:** Give authorized parents and guardians useful learning insights while respecting student privacy.

**Major capabilities:** Learning activity and progress insights, privacy controls, and appropriate permissions.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 16 — Academic & Career Ecosystem

**Purpose:** Extend Beaver AI into broader academic and future-planning support.

**Major capabilities:** Academic test preparation, college planning, and career exploration.

**Status:** Not Started

## Phase 17 — Production, Safety & Scale

**Purpose:** Prepare Beaver AI for safe, reliable, and scalable production use.

**Major capabilities:** Production readiness, safety, reliability, and scalability.

**Status:** Not Started
